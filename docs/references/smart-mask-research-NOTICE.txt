The conversion card explicitly offers the converted weights under MIT. The
original author's model attribution follows the source project's MIT terms:
https://yakhyo.github.io/uniface/license-attribution/
The training dataset, CelebAMask-HQ, restricts its images and derived data to
non-commercial research. Its terms do not explicitly state whether trained
weights are derived data. The MIT grant for the weights and the dataset terms
are distinct; neither alone settles their interaction. As a project precaution,
these weights are included only in local non-commercial research builds and
are removed from Release builds. No weights are included in the source repository.
https://github.com/switchablenorms/CelebAMask-HQ#dataset-agreement

Reviewed 2026-10-31. Current availability, license evidence and packaging gaps:
https://github.com/rankangkang/phosdeck/blob/master/docs/smart-mask-models.md

The saved masks are fixed results. Generation does not identify people across
photographs. Fine boundaries and occluded features need human review.
